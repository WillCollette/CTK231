Apex Legends Ranked

The different ranks of Apex Legends

Apex Legends is a battle royale game that has a ranked system. The ranked system is divided into different tiers, each with its own set of rewards and challenges. The ranks are as follows:

  • Rookie
  • Bronze
  • Silver
  • Gold
  • Platinum
  • Diamond
  • Master
  • Predator

How RP Works

RP, or Ranked Points, is the currency used to determine a player's rank in Apex Legends. Players earn RP by winning matches, getting kills, and completing challenges. The amount of RP earned varies based on the player's performance and the rank of their opponents.

Players can also lose RP if they perform poorly in matches or if they leave matches early. The amount of RP lost varies based on the player's performance and the rank of their opponents.

Players can also earn RP by completing daily and weekly challenges. These challenges can range from getting a certain number of kills to winning a certain number of matches.

Overall, RP is an important aspect of the ranked system in Apex Legends, as it determines a player's rank and rewards. Players should focus on improving their skills and completing challenges to earn more RP and climb the ranks.

Why having a good game sense is important

Having a good game sense is important in Apex Legends because it allows players to make better decisions and react quickly to changing situations. Game sense refers to a player's ability to understand the game mechanics, map layout, and opponent behavior.

Players with good game sense are able to anticipate enemy movements, predict where loot may be located, and make strategic decisions that can lead to victory. They are also able to adapt to different situations and playstyles, which can give them an advantage over less experienced players.

If you dont have a good game sense or good understaning of the game you will not be able to climb the ranks and will be stuck in the lower tiers. Therefore, it is important for players to focus on improving their game sense by practicing, watching tutorials, and learning from experienced players.

How to climb the ranks

Climbing the ranks in Apex Legends takes more than just getting kills. Players need to combine good aim, teamwork, and positioning to perfom well and climb the ranks. Making smart decisions is often more important than getting kills in a match.
